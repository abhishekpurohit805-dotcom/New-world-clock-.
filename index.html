      How this clock works

      Every second the page reads your device’s clock and formats it for the chosen time zone with your browser’s built-in IANA time zone data, so daylight saving changes apply automatically. No location permission is needed. Press / to search any city or time zone.

      Questions

      Does the live clock show seconds?

      Yes. Switch seconds on or off with the Seconds button or the S key.

      Can I use it fullscreen?

      Yes. Press Fullscreen or F. Where your browser supports it, the screen stays awake while fullscreen is open.

      How accurate is the time?

      It shows your device’s clock. Nothing is synced to a server, so if your device clock is off, this page is too.

      Does it need my location?

      No. The time zone comes from your browser settings, and you can change it at any time.

          00:00.000

            Timer

            Count down to any date and time on your device’s clock.
